About Sweet Crumb Bakery

Sweet Crumb Bakery is a fictional local bakery created for our Front-End Development project.

Our concept focuses on fresh products, warm design and a welcoming atmosphere for customers.

We offer artisan bread, handmade pastries and custom cakes. Our goal is to combine traditional baking with modern presentation.

How Our Products Are Made

  1. We select the ingredients.
  2. We prepare the dough.
  3. The dough is rested and shaped.
  4. The products are baked.
  5. They are decorated and prepared for customers.
